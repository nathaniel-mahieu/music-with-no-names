# Comprehension and listening-study protocol

This protocol turns the remaining human-validation gates into concrete sessions. It is not evidence that the studies have already been conducted.

## Consent and privacy

- Explain that this is an educational prototype, not a hearing test.
- Collect no identity beyond an optional participant code.
- Do not upload personal recordings.
- Store model outputs and listener reports in separate columns.
- Obtain explicit consent before saving or aggregating any response.
- Allow withdrawal and deletion without penalty.

Any future cross-listener aggregation must follow the separate [cohort backend proposal](./cohort-backend-proposal.md); this study protocol does not authorize network collection.

## Comprehension session

### Questions before exploration

1. If two frequencies both double, what relationship stays invariant?
2. Can the same ratio sound different in another register or spectrum? Why?
3. Are roughness, harmonicity, fusion, tension, and liking interchangeable?
4. What is the difference between uncertainty before an event and surprise after it?
5. Can an uncomfortable moment contribute to a satisfying whole? Give a mechanism.

### Manipulation tasks

- Predict waveform repetition for 3:2 before revealing the common period.
- Compare sine, harmonic, and stretched spectra at the same fundamentals.
- Identify register, timbre, or timing in the blind counterfactual calibration.
- Compare exact repetition, variation, rupture, and delayed return.
- Explain one Atlas cloud without generalizing it to a genre essence.

### Questions after exploration

Repeat the five conceptual questions, then ask the participant to explain one visualization in their own words and predict the next controlled A/B result. Record correctness and explanation quality with a published rubric before inspecting preference ratings.

## Listening study

Use a within-participant, randomized, level-matched design. Counterbalance A/B order and keep each comparison brief.

Conditions:

- Same ratio; sine, harmonic, and stretched spectra.
- Same spectrum and ratio; low, middle, and high register.
- Same gesture; original, smoothed timbre, doubled register, or +90 ms alternate-event timing.
- Same event materials; exact repetition, variation, unexpected rupture, or delayed return.

For every trial, save these as distinct fields:

- Physical condition and transformation.
- Model roughness, harmonicity, fusion, pulse, uncertainty, and surprise predictions.
- Prediction confidence and model version.
- Listener smoothness, fusion, tension, liking, local comfort, and whole-arc satisfaction.
- Familiarity, device, listening environment, and optional free-text explanation.

Do not fit or revise a model on a participant’s current trial before scoring that trial. Report disagreement and heterogeneity, not only mean correlation.

## Device matrix

Run the core audio flows on current Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Edge where available, using:

- macOS and Windows laptops.
- iOS and Android phones.
- Wired headphones, Bluetooth headphones, laptop speakers, and phone speakers.
- Reduced-motion, high-contrast/forced-color, VoiceOver, and NVDA configurations.

Record codec support, audio-start success, reported latency, audible artifacts, control reachability, focus order, announcements, frame timing, and analysis throughput. The automated accessibility and performance suites are prerequisites, not substitutes.
