# ADR 0004: Visualization contract

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

Every essential visual must expose a textual interpretation and accessible name. Measured evidence, model predictions, curator hypotheses, and listener reports use distinct labels and must not be merged into a single authority-looking score.

## Visual grammar

- Physical frequency uses logarithmic horizontal space where equal distance means equal ratio.
- Time views preserve absolute seconds and declared relative pulse units.
- Model confidence is shown separately from listener disagreement.
- Color is redundant with labels, shape, position, or pattern.
- Conventional pitch-class names are never required to interpret a core visual.
- Resonance Sky keeps performed-note direction fixed in one declared circle-of-fifths orientation so an inferred frame change cannot make the played geometry jump. Its radial coordinate is equal MIDI-key/log-reference-frequency register depth, not the Ratio Lab's measured or authored physical-frequency axis; equal unclamped semitone steps receive equal radial increments, and extreme MIDI values may clamp at the display boundary. A movable Do changes the meridian, relative labels, and hue interpretation—not event identity or position. Color is repeated by route fill/dash state, labels, ring count, line pattern, arrows, and text. Recency and MIDI attack may change bounded opacity or area, but never encode goodness. No ambient motion loops are permitted; event motion must end in the same static geometry exposed under reduced motion.
- Resonance Sky's center must use a deliberately small, persistent glyph grammar. One all-twelve fifths crown distinguishes the learner-selected route from other pitch classes; one circle language distinguishes inside-route, outside-route, and latest attacks; one outer-outline language distinguishes key-down from pedal-sustained sound. Attack density, competing scale candidates, contextual-center cues, route visits, recurrence, chord-root change, motif evidence, and rhythm timing belong in named adjacent readings rather than additional concentric bars, dots, or stars. Phrase-scoped chord hulls must still distinguish interpreted membership from everything sounding, while the attack contour preserves close-time bouquets rather than drawing every attack as one melody. Live sounding fields and historical attack-time snapshots require distinct labels. The SVG description and adjacent text retain bounded evidence that no longer appears as a central ornament.
- Research HUD must remain a separate focus over the same Piano MIDI, movable-Do, route, and newest-twelve-attack state; it may not fork input capture or imply an independent performance. Its foundational coordinate is a fixed chromatic clock where one adjacent position is one semitone. Generator overlays change only connecting paths, never node positions. The thirds lattice must name its `+3` and `+4` axes and diagonal `+7` relation, and the all-pairs matrix must expose every directed modulo-twelve interval in text. Each layout must say what it preserves and folds away. In particular, fifths-path distance is repeated `+7 (mod 12)` motion—not generic affinity, consonance, harmonic function, resolution, emotion, or quality—and every pitch-class projection omits register, voicing, tuning, spectrum, and timing.
- Scale Gravity HUD must remain a separate major-frame focus over that same Piano MIDI, movable Do, live field, and newest-twelve-attack state. Its primary runway contains thirteen equal semitone slots from `0` through the octave copy at `12`; major degrees occupy authored offsets `0·2·4·5·7·9·11`, chromatic gaps remain visible, and recent physical-key movement must not be octave-folded. Native degree controls may pin one pitch while native IV, V, and I controls change only the displayed harmony underneath. Exact 12-TET position, interval, chord membership, and signed MIDI-key movement; style-bound common-practice tendencies; and practitioner or learner listening language must remain three visibly named layers. “Gravity,” color, rub, floating, and arrival may not become ratio-derived scores. Re-homing the seven-note collection rotates its gap order locally without moving pitches or changing the app's selected Do, and must say that a mode requires an established heard home rather than a different starting note alone. The major frame and its seven rotations are culturally bounded teaching coordinates, not a universal source of scales or music.
- The Immersion rhythm panel may project grouped MIDI onsets onto two bars only after the learner supplies BPM, pulses per bar, and a downbeat by locking or starting the metronome. The grid must label itself as declared, keep the unlocked view chronological and beat-free, and never turn nearest-line distance into inferred meter, notation, intention, accuracy, swing, groove, quality, or felt experience. A close-time bouquet occupies one timing mark with an attack count.
- The Immersion lower-right scale panel must keep one semitone cell per equal-key pitch class from selected Do, so physical spacing remains primary. Route degree, recent-five position, newest attack, and currently sounding state require redundant fill, number, marker, outline, and text. The selected route name, cyclic gap loop, exact latest position from Do, retained route coverage, and locked/discovery state remain visible. This panel repeats the learner's working coordinate; it must not become key detection, route-fit confidence, correctness, tonal function, resolution, or quality.
- The same scale panel may expose exactly two quick reset actions. Next-note Do capture must retain the selected route, lock after one new attack, and share state with the persistent route control. Frame reset must mark the current event boundary, unlock discovery, remove pinned URL coordinates, and exclude all earlier phrase events from subsequent frame evidence without deleting the phrase. Visible live text must count distinct post-reset pitch classes and state the four-note minimum. Re-arming either action cancels the other; no old and new evidence window may remain silently active together.
- The scale panel may show one small ascending octave fingering strip after performed register evidence exists. It realizes the selected route in the nearest played octave, chooses left hand only when that octave is centered below middle C, and otherwise chooses right hand. Route syllables, finger digits, hand, octave/register cause, and crossing point must survive without color and in one complete text alternative. The strip is a bounded route-count practice heuristic, not observed technique or a universal fingering across keys, directions, hands, and phrases.
- Immersion history is one shared newest-twelve-attack projection over the longer Piano phrase. Notes, route visits, local recurrence/center evidence, relationship-window evidence, and chord grouping must receive that same slice. Any smaller chord-membrane count is a rendering budget inside the shared window and must not silently become a different chord-history definition.
- The standalone Voice lab must keep microphone input activity, accepted acoustic-pitch evidence, and declared piano reference coordinates separate. The learner explicitly starts and stops access and independently chooses the anchor. One continuously labeled marker may show a supported monophonic fundamental against a declared target; adjacent semitone segments, signed cents, detected hertz, nearest-key reference hertz, target hertz, periodicity clarity, and an uncalibrated input-activity meter must also remain available in text. Color may reinforce below/at/above target but cannot carry direction alone. Low-level or weakly periodic input disappears rather than becoming a guessed note, while the activity meter can still confirm arriving samples. The page must state that audio is not recorded or uploaded and that it does not recognize words or measure connected-piano tuning, vocal correctness, quality, identity, emotion, preference, or learning.
- The selected Piano scale route must remain visible outside any one focus lens. Its human-readable name, ordered semitone-gap loop, cumulative octave positions from Do, and locked/discovery state are required text. Direct selection must use a native control, retain the current Do, and lock the selected route; unlocking must restore evidence-led frame discovery; adopting a compatible candidate may change Do and route together. The interface must explicitly distinguish the fifths compass as a Do selector that retains the route. No route state may be encoded only by color or inferred from the circle graphic.
- A persistent native action must let the learner assign the next new MIDI or on-screen attack as movable Do from every Piano focus. Arming and cancellation require visible pressed-state and polite-status text. Capture changes one pitch-class coordinate, locks but does not replace the selected route, updates the URL, and reinterprets all degree/context views together; the same attack remains measured MIDI evidence. Any frozen exercise bound to the former center must reset rather than silently mixing reference frames. This action is a learner assignment, never tonic or key detection.
- An exact chord in the Immersion harmony lens must state inversion beside the chord name when its interpreted bass is not the catalog root. The supporting line must name the bass, root, inversion ordinal, and chord quality; root position must explicitly identify root and bass as the same pitch class. Inversion derives from root-relative template order and cannot be inferred from sorted absolute pitch classes or encoded only in the membrane geometry.
- A detected exact chord may add one compact realized-keyboard fingering graphic only when its physical voicing fits two through five distinct keys within one octave. Every finger digit must appear on its physical key and in the SVG text alternative; the visible label must name the hand and define thumb/pinky endpoints. The normal-keyboard allocation uses the realized voicing midpoint: below middle C suggests left hand, while middle C or higher suggests right. The suggestion must not imply an observed fingering, ergonomic certainty, fixed hand territories, or a universal choice independent of hand size, black keys, crossing, accompaniment, register, and neighboring chords.

## Consequences

A compact graphic without a textual interpretation is unfinished. A scalar called “goodness,” “consonance,” or “emotion” is prohibited unless its listener, purpose, inputs, uncertainty, and components are visible.
