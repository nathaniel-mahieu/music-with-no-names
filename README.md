# Music With No Names

An audible, visual exploration of music through frequency ratios, spectra, time, perception, and expectation—without treating note names or piano geometry as the fundamental objects.

Public site: [nathaniel-mahieu.github.io/music-with-no-names](https://nathaniel-mahieu.github.io/music-with-no-names/)

The current milestone is **Interval Context Echo v1.56**. It provides:

- Safe, user-initiated two-tone synthesis.
- A companion-first learning hierarchy: Start, the live Piano companion, and the learner's Personal Lens stay prominent while specialist pitch, time, listening, recording, and landmark labs live in one grouped disclosure instead of competing as eleven equal destinations.
- A persistent return-to-phrase cue outside Piano whenever the current tab contains live attacks. It reports the phrase size and active learning lens, preserves the movable-Do frame and URL state, and returns to the same silent phrase without turning its measurements into a score.
- A phrase-first Ear bridge that carries the latest two Piano attacks into one controlled question: which conclusions survive when only the assumed sound changes? Equal-key motion, frequency ratio, absolute fundamentals, attack spacing, and release-proven overlap remain measured MIDI facts. Only a proven overlap opens four declared spectrum models—sine, exact harmonic, mellow-piano proxy, and bright-piano proxy—whose friction, harmonic fit, and fusion hypotheses stay separate from the learner's unclaimed experience. A sequential interval receives no simultaneous spectral-interaction result, and the generated nine-control hearing instrument now waits in an optional disclosure.
- A phrase-first Harmony bridge that carries the latest two compact attacked fields from Piano, including the learner's chord-timing and boundary corrections. It draws the hand-to-hand transformation, names interval landmarks and nearest-key voice motion, then keeps fixed-spectrum sensory changes, conditional musical affordances, and the learner's unclaimed experience in separate lenses. Held or pedaled inheritance, tonal pull, actual keyboard audio, and emotion are not silently reconstructed; the generated Harmony instrument now waits in an optional disclosure.
- A phrase-first Journey bridge that turns the retained MIDI phrase into onset-group field shapes, bass movement, and relative-time steps. Repeated gestures accumulate only phrase-local continuation evidence, so the learner can see the exact event where a continuation becomes known, where a new branch appears, and where the model still has no precedent. Chords inside 70 ms become one event; the generated sixteen-second demonstration remains available in an optional disclosure.
- A phrase-first Rhythm bridge that removes pitch identity from the retained MIDI phrase, clusters chord attacks before measuring time, adopts the median onset gap as an explicitly local ruler, and shows exact normalized gap lengths beside nearby ratio landmarks. Recurrent gap shapes, MIDI attack range, sounding overlap, silence, and missing release evidence stay separate; the bridge does not infer a beat, meter, groove, intention, or quality. The former twelve-position authoring instrument now waits in an optional disclosure.
- A live Phrase-to-Atlas bridge that places the retained MIDI phrase beside one selected, curator-authored song landmark on three separate coordinates: relationship recurrence, piece-local transition surprise, and timing activity. The comparison is an invitation to listen for resemblance, not a song match, style detector, ranking, emotion inference, or goodness score; broader curator concepts such as embodied drive remain explicitly non-equivalent to the narrower MIDI proxy.
- An intention-led whole-phrase experiment: before freezing phrase A, choose transposition, timing, touch, articulation, one interval, or the ending as the single coordinate to investigate. Phrase B then separates whether that coordinate moved, whether its declared control condition stayed intact, which other lenses also crossed their display thresholds, and which remained invariant—without calling the replay correct or causal.
- A live two-note partial-interaction microscope inside Interval practice. It pairs the selected sounding note with its nearest sounding neighbor, plots both assumed partial combs on one log-frequency coordinate, connects close alignment within 18 cents, and distinguishes the strongest nearby interaction zones from exact overlap. Roughness still aggregates every cross-partial contribution, including weak pairs omitted from the six-link display, while overlap remains a separate proxy. MIDI supplies only the fundamentals: every upper partial and interaction link comes from the selected teaching spectrum, not the keyboard, DAW, room, or listener, and neither output is musical goodness.

A matched replay keeps relationship, sound, motion, selected-Do context, and listener experience in five separate lenses: equal-key span and ratio may survive while register, hertz gap, attack gap, direction, overlap, surrounding field, and relative roles change. Simultaneous roughness and partial-overlap models appear only when release evidence proves that both source and echo overlapped; melodic or release-unknown pairs receive no fabricated spectral comparison. ## Product principle

Music is represented twice:

1. As invariant relationships such as frequency and duration ratios.
2. As an embodied realization in hertz, seconds, spectra, loudness, and register.

No acoustic metric is presented as an objective measure of whether music is good.
